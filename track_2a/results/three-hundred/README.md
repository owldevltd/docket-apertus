# Run: 300 documents through the chain

The run behind the entry "How many of 300 documents run through without a mark, at every station of the chain?" in
`docs/tests.md` and behind the headline figure of the technical report.

- `result.md` – the share of documents without a mark for a person, with intervals and per language, the course over
  the shuffled order, every station of the chain, what holds the documents with a mark, and the question across the
  parliaments counted on the same documents.
- `draw.json` – the 300 documents in the order of the run, each with its set and its OpenParlData ids (the shipped
  ones by their folder in `data/documents/`).

The documents, the values and the calls are not kept here: the documents are not in the repository
(`data/documents/README.md`).
